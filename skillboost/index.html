Ask us anything
Which program should I start with?

Start with the gap that costs you most. If you hesitate in meetings, begin with communication. If you feel like an accidental leader, begin with behavioral. Not sure? Book a consult and we'll map it for you.

Are the programs live or self-paced?

It depends on the program: online cohorts, live interactive workshops, live classes with 1-on-1 feedback, framework-driven bootcamps and micro-learning. Each card lists its exact format.

I'm an introvert — will I have to become someone else?

No. The tech programs are built so you don't need to change your personality to lead — they turn presence and stakeholder interaction into logical, step-by-step systems that feel authentic.

Do I need to wear a suit?

Not at all. The Tech Executive Wardrobe shows how to look like a tech executive while staying comfortable and true to tech culture.

How long does each program take?

From 15 days for the appearance programs up to 8 weeks for The Fearless Speaker Blueprint.
